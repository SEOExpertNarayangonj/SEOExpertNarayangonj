FAQ

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO helps a business appear in Google Maps and local search results when nearby customers search for its services.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) help your business get found and cited in AI-powered answers, such as AI Overviews and chat-based search.

How long does Local SEO take?

It depends on competition, location and the current state of your profile and website. Many businesses see movement in a few months, but I never guarantee specific rankings.

Do you work with ecommerce stores?

Yes. I work with Shopify, WooCommerce and Magento stores on category, product page and technical SEO.
